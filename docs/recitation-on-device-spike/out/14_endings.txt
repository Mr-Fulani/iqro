# What a stop is: a fifth of a second of silence, or more, after the last sound heard of a word. If the next word
# begins before any such silence, the reader reads on, and the sounds a stop would leave unsaid or change are
# asked for as written.

# The harness itself was wrong since PR #307: of the sounds it changes in the text to see whether the change is
# noticed, it took the first of a word, and that is now often a sound that only a stop adds (the hamzah a word
# begins with anew), which is never asked for. So "a short vowel changed: 90 of 162" (out/13_long_words.txt) was
# mostly the harness; it now changes only sounds that reading on always has.
